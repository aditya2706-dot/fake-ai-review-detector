# Product photography credits

Every image in this directory is a photograph from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com),
used under the [Unsplash Licence](https://unsplash.com/license) (free to use,
commercially or otherwise, no permission needed).

They were chosen so that **no real brand is legible** in any of them. That is a
hard requirement, not an aesthetic one: every product in this store carries a
fabricated review history and a fabricated manipulation score, and hanging those
off a photograph of a real company's product would attach invented fraud to a real
business. The product names and brands in `src/frd/data/products.json` are
invented for the same reason.
